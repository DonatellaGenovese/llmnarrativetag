OBSERVABLE GLOSSARY
===================

m  —  jet mass (GeV)
  Definition: Ungroomed jet mass.
  Meaning:    The invariant mass of the whole jet. A hadronically decaying top quark deposits its decay products in one jet, so the mass concentrates near the top mass; QCD jets acquire mass only from radiation and sit lower.

m_SD  —  soft-drop mass (GeV)
  Definition: Soft-drop mass (z_cut = 0.1, beta = 0, R0 = 0.8).
  Meaning:    The jet mass after soft radiation has been groomed away; with beta = 0 the z_cut condition is angle-independent, so soft emissions are removed at all angles and only the hard core survives. Less sensitive to pile-up and underlying event than the ungroomed mass.

tau21  —  N-subjettiness ratio tau2/tau1
  Definition: N-subjettiness ratio tau2/tau1 (beta = 1, one-pass kT axes).
  Meaning:    The residual left by describing the radiation with two axes, relative to one. Low values indicate a genuinely two-pronged jet; values near one mean the second axis buys nothing.

tau32  —  N-subjettiness ratio tau3/tau2
  Definition: N-subjettiness ratio tau3/tau2 (beta = 1, one-pass kT axes).
  Meaning:    The residual left by three axes relative to two. Low values indicate three resolved prongs, the configuration expected from a top decaying to three quarks, but the ratio is only meaningful once the jet has appreciable mass.

C2_double_b1  —  energy correlation ratio C2
  Definition: Energy correlation ratio C2 (beta = 1).
  Meaning:    A double ratio of energy correlation functions, e3*e1/e2^2, using up to the three-point correlator; the subscript counts prongs, so low values favour two-pronged structure. Strongly correlated with jet mass and pT, so it must not be read independently of them.

D2_double_b1  —  energy correlation ratio D2
  Definition: Energy correlation ratio D2 (beta = 1).
  Meaning:    e3*e1^3/e2^3, built by power counting to separate two-pronged from one-pronged jets with the mass correlation of C2 largely removed. Low values favour two prongs specifically, not multi-prong in general, so it is not the natural discriminant for a three-pronged top decay.

C3_double_b1  —  energy correlation ratio C3
  Definition: Energy correlation ratio C3 (beta = 1, truncated at 50 constituents).
  Meaning:    The three-prong analogue of C2, e4*e2/e3^2, built from correlators up to four points, with low values favouring a third resolved energy centre. Computed on at most 50 constituents, so high-multiplicity jets are systematically biased.

N3_b1  —  generalized correlator N3
  Definition: Generalized correlator N3 (beta = 1, truncated at 40 constituents).
  Meaning:    A ratio of generalized energy correlators, which retain only a subset of the pairwise angles and so give better power counting in the three-prong region and less mass correlation than C3. Low values favour three-pronged structure; computed on at most 40 constituents, so high-multiplicity jets are systematically biased.

pT  —  jet transverse momentum (GeV)
  Definition: Jet transverse momentum.
  Meaning:    The momentum of the jet transverse to the beam. It sets the scale of the jet rather than its substructure, and fixes how collimated the decay products of a heavy particle would be.

n_const  —  constituent multiplicity
  Definition: Constituent multiplicity.
  Meaning:    How many particles the jet contains. Multiplicity grows with the amount of radiation in the shower and with the number of hard partons initiating it.
